# StockSense Demo Flow

When presenting the StockSense Hackathon project to the judges, follow this exact sequence to demonstrate the robust double-entry inventory engine and modular features.

### Step 1: Login
- Log into the application as a Warehouse Manager to showcase the authentication and RBAC boundary.

### Step 2: Dashboard Overview
- Land on the main dashboard. Point out the Analytics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

### Step 3: Product Setup
- Navigate to the Products page. 
- Create or display the **Steel Rod (RAW-STL-001)** product.

### Step 4: Incoming Receipt
- Execute a **Receipt** for 100 kg of Steel Rods from a supplier into the Main Warehouse.

### Step 5: Verify Stock Increase
- Navigate to Current Stock. 
- Show that the stock level for Steel Rods has correctly increased to 100 kg.

### Step 6: Internal Transfer
- Execute an **Internal Transfer** moving the 100 kg of Steel Rods from the Main Warehouse to the Production Rack.

### Step 7: Verify Location Change
- Show the Current Stock again. 
- Prove that the total global stock remains 100 kg, but the specific location has shifted accurately.

### Step 8: Outgoing Delivery
- Process a **Delivery Order** for 20 kg of Steel Rods to a customer.

### Step 9: Stock Adjustment
- Process a **Stock Adjustment** for 3 kg of damaged/missing Steel Rods.

### Step 10: The Single Source of Truth
- Open the **Stock Ledger**. 
- Emphasize that every single movement (Receipt, Transfer, Delivery, Adjustment) is logged chronologically as append-only entries, totaling exactly 77 kg on hand.

### Step 11: Analytics Dashboard
- Show the Analytics mini-dashboard, visualizing the real-time stock movement trends generated by the ledger.
